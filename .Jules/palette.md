## 2024-06-18 - Missing ARIA Labels in Password Modal\n**Learning:** Icon-only buttons within modals (specifically password visibility toggles and close buttons) frequently lack ARIA labels and focus-visible states across the component library.\n**Action:** Add `aria-label` and `focus-visible:ring-2` (with `outline-none` and `rounded-full` or `rounded-lg`) consistently to all icon-only interactive elements to ensure screen reader and keyboard accessibility.
